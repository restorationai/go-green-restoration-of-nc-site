## Restoration emergencies common in Clayton

Clayton's growth has outpaced a lot of its older infrastructure, and that mismatch shows up in restoration calls. Summer thunderstorms and the remnants of Atlantic hurricanes dump heavy rain fast, and with Johnston County's clay-heavy soil draining slowly, water backs up against foundations and finds its way into crawlspaces faster than homeowners expect. Winter brings hard overnight freezes that split exposed or poorly insulated supply lines, especially in homes built before modern code required deeper insulation in unconditioned spaces. Add in the humidity that sits over central North Carolina for most of the year, and any slow leak that goes unnoticed for even a few days has a real shot at turning into a mold problem.

## Services we provide in Clayton

Water damage in Clayton often starts in the crawlspace rather than a visible room, since so much of the town's housing stock, old and new, sits on raised foundations instead of slabs. We pull standing water, pull back insulation that's holding moisture against the subfloor, and dry the space per the IICRC S500 standard before it becomes a structural issue.

Storm damage tends to follow the same pattern every hurricane season: wind-driven rain finds a weak point in older roofing, or a tree limb opens up a section of roof decking during a tropical system passing through. We tarp, extract, and start drying before the next band of rain arrives.

Fire and smoke damage calls in Clayton run the gamut from kitchen fires in newer builds to older homes where smoke has worked its way into plaster and original woodwork. Soot and smoke odor behave differently depending on what burned and what it settled into, which changes how we clean versus how we seal and repaint.

Mold remediation is frequently the second call after a water loss that sat too long, or a standalone call from a crawlspace that's been holding moisture quietly for a season. We contain the area, remove affected materials, and address the moisture source so it doesn't come back.
